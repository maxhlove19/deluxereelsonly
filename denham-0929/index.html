A note on this week

These are late and that is on me, not on anything at your end. They should have been with you Tuesday morning.

Two things you may want to know, since they affected which moments I picked:

  • Your camera cuts to the congregation partway through several stretches on Wednesday. Two passages I wanted are unusable because the clip would have been the back of your head and a pew of faces. If your operator can hold on the pulpit through a story, those stretches become the best clips of the week.
  • One Sunday morning passage sits over the projected verse slide. A vertical clip crops the sides off a full-frame slide and the verse becomes unreadable, so I used a different moment from the same message.

All six are from your own preaching, as you asked back in September.
