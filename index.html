Questions

The real ones.

Is the free edit actually free?

Yes. You send a video, I cut it, I send it back. No card, no trial that starts billing, nothing to cancel. If you don't like it, you keep the edit and we're done.

Pack or monthly — which one?

If you have a backlog or post a few times a week, a pack: 30 for $300, or 50 for $500 with five extra, use them at your own pace, nothing expires. If you're making content every day and want someone on it every day, the full-time monthly at $750 — unlimited. Not sure? Start with a pack; you can move to monthly whenever it starts making sense.

How fast is the turnaround really?

First cut within 48 hours of you sending the footage. Revisions come back the same day you ask for them. If a week ever goes quiet, something has gone wrong and you should chase me.

Do you write the hooks or do I?

I do, from what you actually said. I won't put words in your mouth — the hook gets built out of the strongest sentence already in the footage.

What do you need from me to start?

A link to one video. That's the whole thing. No brand deck, no questionnaire, no kickoff call.

Do I have to get on a call?

No. Everything runs over email. If you'd rather talk we can, but nothing about this requires it and you'll never be chased for one.

Who's actually doing the editing?

Me — Max. I've been editing video since 2016. It isn't outsourced and it isn't a queue. If the work is good that's mine, and if it's late that's mine too.
